Capacity to Deadline Optimizer

Frontier Specialty Care Network (fictional) · 13,951 appointments and 1,786 waitlist entries · 6 months. Average lead time is 29.8 days and only 59.1% of appointments meet their specialty's access standard. Meanwhile 2,087 booked slots went unused through cancellation and no-show, and 507 of them were cancelled early enough to refill.

Fully synthetic data · no employer, patient or confidential information

How this page is built. Every value comes from a SQL query stored in dashboard/dashboard_config.json and run against data/scheduling.db by src/build_dashboard.py. Nothing is typed in by hand, so the dashboard cannot drift away from the analysis in sql/. Rebuild with python src/load_sqlite.py && python src/build_dashboard.py.

Limitations. The fill simulation applies a flat refill rate to cancellations with enough notice; it assumes a matching patient is reachable and willing, which is the assumption most likely to break in practice. Waitlist match score weights are judgement, not fitted to acceptance outcomes. Lead time is calendar days from request to appointment and ignores patient-driven delay, so some of it is preference rather than access failure. The data is fully synthetic.

Source: synthetic data generated by src/generate_data.py, seed 5150. No employer data, patient information, protected health information or confidential business information is used. Patient_Group is a synthetic cohort label, not a person.
